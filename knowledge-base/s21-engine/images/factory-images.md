# Factory manual page images

These are single pages from the factory manuals, reduced to greyscale JPGs. They are personal reference copies of the pages cited in `../factory-service-data.md`, `../electrics.md` and `../fuel-and-conditions.md`. Whole PDFs are **not** stored in the repo.

Page numbering:
- **pr. p.** is the number printed on the page.
- **PDF p.** is the page number in the archive.org PDF viewer.
- **file** is the JPG number in the local scan folder.
