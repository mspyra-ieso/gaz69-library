# S-21 in a GAZ-69 (`GAZ-69 z silnikiem S-21`)

## Background

- In Poland, most S-21s found in GAZ-69s were fitted **during factory general overhauls** (`NG`, naprawa główna) at designated workshops, in place of the original side-valve engine (`dolniak`). Owners consider the S-21 (`górniak`) stronger and more economical. [web article] https://laspolski.pl/2311-gaz-69-auto-z-dusza/
- The S-21 is a Polish development of the M-20. It has the same 82 × 100 mm bore and stroke, about 70 hp versus 52 to 57 hp, and the same mounting points as the M-20 / GAZ-21 engines. [FORUM] Russian owners' summary via search of https://www.drive2.ru/l/573142427284540104/ and the gaz69.ru FAQ thread http://www.gaz69.ru/ipb/topic/107548-faq-%D0%BF%D0%BE-s-21-nysa/ (this page could not be read fully; it is login-gated or truncated)

## Carb, air filter and intake

- S-21 manifolds were made for **single-choke and twin-choke** carbs. [FORUM] drive2 summary
- GAZ-69 owners have used a Jikov single-choke, a DAAZ 2107 (Lada) twin-choke with re-jetting, or the Weber. One owner lapped the carb flange and made an air-cleaner bracket from an AZLK-2141 part. Another fitted a Renault Scénic dry filter. [FORUM] https://oldpolbus.com/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=101 ; drive2 573142427284540104 ; drive2 509669270280470698
- Throttle: a cable worked poorly in one install, so the owner made a rod linkage from Weber parts. [FORUM] oldpolbus t=101
- Crankcase breather: open on the S-21. One owner added an oil separator for a closed system. The breather hose must go to the correct carb fitting. [FORUM] drive2 573142427284540104

## Running experience

- About 10 to 14 L/100 km. Torquey, idles low, louder at high rpm. One owner keeps it under about 2100 rpm (personal choice). [FORUM] drive2 posts; oldpolbus t=101
- Used engines sold as "after overhaul" are often only partially rebuilt, so check before buying. [FORUM] Duplet59, drive2 509669270280470698
- One owner's S-21 used a lot of oil. [FORUM] BoBiKkz comment, drive2 509669270280470698

## Gaps

No dedicated Polish swap thread was found. No measured drawings exist for an adapter plate. The evidence suggests none is needed, because the engine bolts to the GAZ bellhousing as an M-20 replacement, but **verify on your vehicle**. Exhaust downpipe fitment was not documented.
