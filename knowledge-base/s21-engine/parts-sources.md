## Shops and places to look

- **Regmot** (sklep.regmot.com.pl / regmot24.pl): wide S-21 range
- **Techmot24** (techmot24.pl): Caring gaskets, modules
- **Roda24** (sklep.roda24.pl): Żuk/Nysa parts
- **Auto-Truck** (auto-truck.pl): Warszawa/Żuk/UAZ/GAZ
- **Autogama** (autogama.pl)
- **RETRO 69 / gaz69.eu**: GAZ-69 parts, including the M-20/S-21/ZMZ-21 oil filter adapter
- **Tarmot 4x4** (sklep.tarmot4x4.pl): rebuilt GAZ-69 engines and transfer cases
- **Zabytkowe Części** (zabytkoweczesci.pl): used parts
- Allegro and OLX: search "aparat zapłonowy żuk", "S21"
- Forum tip: oil pump repair kits that include the gears are rare. Buy one when you see it. [blog] https://zukiemprzezswiat.pl/remont-silnika-2/

Shared parts: M-20, GAZ-21 and GAZ-69 distributors fit (see ignition file). Many UAZ/M-20 ancillaries also fit. [FORUM] https://www.drive2.ru/l/509669270280470698/
