# S-21: Ignition Upgrades and Common Mods

## Electronic ignition options (`zapłon elektroniczny`, `bezstykowy`)

| Option | Details | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Factory S-21 electronic distributor** | Late-production Żuks (about 1993 to 1995, catalyst versions) had a factory contactless distributor with a "fin" (`płetwa`) on the shaft end. These are rare and expensive new. The module is **APE 05**, which replaces Unitra/Telpod GL-118 and Hybryd H-161 and also fits Polonez 1.5/1.6 GLE. | [FORUM] Marian, buźka, https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=7&t=3936 ; [shop] https://techmot24.pl/modul-zaplonowy-elektroniczny-fso-1500-e-polonez,3,22226,34001 |
| **Polonez 1.5 distributor transplant** | Cut and notch the Polonez distributor shaft and join it to the Żuk distributor's lower shaft. Transfer the Żuk clamp (`obejma`) and timing scale. Use a **1.5 donor** (same advance springs as the Żuk). Nysa needs an angled cap. Donor about 30 to 50 zł. | [FORUM] Strucelsw, t=3936 |
| **Keep the points, add a module** | The old large Unitra FSO module can be triggered by the points. Bridge outputs B and G with about **270 Ω / 2 W**. GL-118 and H-161 **cannot** be used this way. The GL-200 works with a 4226 coil without resistors (Marian). | [FORUM] OLO, Strucelsw, Marian, t=3936 |
| **Coil choice** | Strucelsw advises against the **4226** coil ("wide spread", misfires) and recommends the **Bosch VW 0 221 122 349**, tested on a scope. With points, do **not** use a low-primary-resistance electronic coil, because it overheats. | [FORUM] t=3936; elektroda search summary https://poszukaj.elektroda.pl/szukaj,zap%C5%82on-%C5%BCuk.html |
| **Lada (Zhiguli) contactless kit** | Used by Russian GAZ-69 S-21 owners. About 2 h of angle-grinder adaptation. | [FORUM] https://oldpolbus.com/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=101 ; Duplet59, https://www.drive2.ru/l/573142427284540104/ |
| 1993 Żuk with factory Polonez-type module | That module **will not work with points**. | [FORUM via search] https://www.elektroda.pl/rtvforum/topic2587035.html |
| Fuel injection later? | Needs contactless ignition first, because it needs a trigger signal. | [FORUM] https://www.elektroda.pl/rtvforum/topic3689029.html (search summary) |

Note: one forum admin (Marian) owns a new electronic unit but still prefers points ("analog") ignition. Points are simple and fixable at the roadside. If you convert, carry the old distributor as a spare.

Timing after conversion: not discussed in the sources. Re-time with a strobe.
