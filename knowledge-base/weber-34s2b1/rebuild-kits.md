## Idle cut-off solenoid (elektrozawór) and jets

- **[FORUM]** The solenoid is the same body as on the Fiat 126p and Polonez Webers. Only the idle jet on its tip differs: 45 for the Żuk. Thread M8×1.
- **[FORUM]** A plain jet holder (śruba) can replace it; the jets interchange with the holder.
- **[FORUM, Marian]** Don't break working solenoids, because they are scarce.
- Source: https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=4635

## Quality warnings

- **Check the number cast on the body before buying.** 34S2B1, 34S2A1 and 28/35 S2A1 parts differ.
  - ETER-MOT labels the 34S2A1/34S2B1 kit "Żuk ST" but the 34S2B1 carburettor "NT", which is inconsistent. Ask the seller.
- **Floats:**
  - The ETER-MOT kit has no float.
  - [FORUM] Plastic floats can absorb fuel. https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?p=44947
- **Needle valves:**
  - Prefer one with a spring damper. https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=3139
  - [FORUM] Needles with rubber tips are common. https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=5245 (snippet)
- **New-old-stock and "new" Jikov carburettors** were unreliable according to [FORUM] users. This is not about the Weber, but it shows that "new" does not mean "works". https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=777
