# Sources (technical track)

Type tags:
- **FACTORY:** the vehicle maker's manual.
- **BOOK:** a technical book.
- **SITE:** an enthusiast article.
- **FORUM:** a forum thread.
- **SHOP:** a parts seller listing.

## Factory manuals and books (archive.org, uploaded from zukowka.wordpress.com)

- https://archive.org/download/fsc-lublin-instrukcja-obslugi-zuk/FSC_LUBLIN-1985-instrukcja_obslugi_Zuk.pdf
  - FACTORY. The key 34S2B1 source.
  - States the S-21 uses the "34S2B1, produkcji FOS-Łódź", with a mechanical progressive secondary.
  - Contains the full calibration table (4.1), float 5–5.5 mm and travel 8.5 mm, the idle procedure (600–800 rpm), gauge 17-P-1107, the fault table, and the exploded view.
- https://archive.org/download/fsc-lublin-instrukcja-obslugi-zuk/FSC_LUBLIN-1989-instrukcja_obslugi_Zuk.pdf
  - FACTORY. 28/35 S2A with EZ-20 solenoid: full table, float procedure, solenoid get-you-home trick.
- https://archive.org/download/fsc-lublin-instrukcja-obslugi-zuk/FSC_LUBLIN-1994-instrukcja_obslugi_Zuk.pdf
  - FACTORY. 28/35 S2B1: full table including the economiser, float 7 ±0.25 mm without gasket, sealed mixture screw, cleaning interval, warning not to swap the I/II jets.
- https://archive.org/download/fsc-lublin-instrukcja-obslugi-zuk/FSC_LUBLIN-1974-instrukcja_obslugi_Zuk.pdf (OCR text `..._djvu.txt` used)
  - FACTORY. Shows the 1974 Żuk used the Jikov 40 SOP, i.e. before the Weber.
- https://archive.org/download/zsd-1977-instrukcja-obslugi-nysa-522/ZSD-1977-instrukcja_obslugi_Nysa_522.pdf
  - FACTORY. Nysa 522 with "WEBER 34S2B1" or Jikov 40 SOP.
  - Weber data: main jets 1.20/1.50, idle jets 0.50, venturi 25.
  - Weber idle 550–650 rpm; ignition 7° BTDC; Fiat 125p-type dry filter.
- https://archive.org/download/zukowka-zuk-spare-parts-catalog/ZUK-BEN-angielski_djvu.txt
  - BOOK. Wiejak & Zagaja, *Żuk – budowa, eksploatacja, naprawa* (1992 edition), unofficial English machine translation.
  - Circuit descriptions (main, idle and progression, pump, starter, secondary linkage).
  - Table 2.18 for 28/35 S2A1, 28/35 S2B1 and 34S2C16.
  - Starter-cable adjustment, PCV valve linked to the throttle, mounting stack.
- https://archive.org/download/zukowka-zuk-spare-parts-catalog_202204/FSC_LUBLIN-1982-katalog_czesci_zamiennych_samochodu_Zuk_djvu.txt
  - FACTORY parts catalogue 1982. Confirms "Gaźnik dwuprzelotowy WEBER, typ 34S2B1" (OCR shows "345281"), and Weber "standard" and "tropik" engine versions.
  - The OCR is too poor to extract part numbers.
- https://archive.org/advancedsearch.php (search for zukowka / Żuk manuals)
  - Used to find the items above.

## Web pages

- https://www.elektroda.pl/rtvforum/topic3976154.html (read via https://web.archive.org/web/20241206175013/https://www.elektroda.pl/rtvforum/topic3976154.html because the live site timed out)
  - FORUM.
  - Trzeciak 1989 book data for "Fos 34S2A1": venturi 25/25, main 125/145, air 210/210, emulsion F30/F30.
  - Claim that the 34S2A1 has a vacuum-controlled secondary; mention of 34S2A2 for Żuk/Nysa.
- https://polskiedostawczaki.pl/podstawowe-regulacje-silnikow-s-21-oraz-m-20/
  - SITE. Float distances for 28/35 S2A1, 28/35 S2B1 and 34S2A1/B1 (5.0–5.5 mm).
  - Idle procedure (mixture screw 2 turns out).
  - Book scans (Wiejak & Zagaja figures) used as images.
- https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=3998
  - FORUM. Nysa with 28/35 S2A1 (no variant named in some posts).
  - Measured jets 118/145; recommended 110, idle 45 in the solenoid, F27/250 and F35/225/145; S2B1 secondary 125.
  - Float measured at 8.5 mm then reset to 6.5 mm; CO and lambda results; pump-nozzle siphoning; swapped emulsion tubes.
- https://zlombol.pl/community/technikalia-2/gaznik-weber-28-35-s2a-zuk-problem-2/
  - FORUM. 28/35 S2A "ver.1/ver.3", version stamped on the body, emulsion tube markings 6/2 versus 5/3 and 5/4.
  - "Factory 145" jet claim; economy experiments with 135/115 jets.
