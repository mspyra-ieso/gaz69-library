# Owner experiences: symptom → what fixed it

All entries are **forum opinion or experience**, paraphrased in English. Each has a short original Polish quote (under 15 words). "Variant" means the carburettor model named in the post. "Weber (unspecified)" means the poster named no model, but the vehicle is an S-21 Żuk or Nysa.

Almost everything here comes from forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl (FMPD), the main Polish forum for Żuk and Nysa vans. Full page texts are archived outside the repository; see `sources-troubleshooting.md`.

| # | Vehicle / variant | Symptom | What fixed it / outcome | Polish original (short quote) | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Żuk A, Weber (unspecified), 2010 | After cleaning: started instantly but shook, black smoke, all plugs fouled, fuel spitting up into the air filter. The mixture screw was fully in, and backing it out stalled the engine. | The owner had **fitted the jets the wrong way round**. Correcting them gave smoother running and lower consumption. He then found a damaged fuel-pump diaphragm and replaced it. | "miałem wkręcone odwrotnie dysze"; "gaźnik wręcz wypluwa benzynę do góry" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=777&start=15 , https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=777&start=30 |
| 2 | Weber 28/35 S2A on another engine, 2019–20 | After cleaning: fuel poured from the jets on every throttle press and the engine would not start. The solenoid tip had been snapped off. | The tip *was* the **idle jet** ("Zepsułeś"). The final cure for the no-start was a **second deep clean**: two rounds of soaking and heating dislodged debris from the channels. | "z kanałów wyleciało jeszcze mnóstwo syfu, które musiały blokować przepływ" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=4635 , https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=4635&start=15 |
| 3 | Same thread, member "zolwik", same carburettor type | Cleaned before Christmas, now won't start. | Not reported. | "To jakaś epidemia na gaźniki" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=4635 |
| 4 | Żuk A15B S-21, Weber (unspecified), 2020 | Black, dry, sooty plugs and misfire after standing. Fuel under the manifold. | **Needle valve stuck open.** Cleaning it cured the idle stumble, but it still ran rich. Advice: check the float, set the level to about 6.5 mm, renew the idle jet (45) and the solenoid O-ring (6×2 mm), set the mixture screw 0.5–1.25 turns out, and use Denso plugs. | "Zaworek iglicowy był zacięty w pozycji otwartej"; "zmień na nową dysze paliwa i oring elektrozaworu 6x2mm" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=4814 |
| 5 | Same thread, sukiennik68 | Choke has to come off within seconds of starting. | Suspects primary and secondary main jets swapped. | "zamienione dysze I i II przelotu" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=4814 |
| 6 | Nysa S-21, Weber 28/35 S2A1, 2018 | CO above 7%, black smoke after a blip, fuel dripping when hand-pumping, idle jet clogged twice. | **New needle valve** stopped the leak. Primary main jet changed from 118 to 110, float reset from 8.5 to 6.5 mm, new air filter. Readings improved further after a brake servo and master cylinder were replaced. Idle CO 2.4%. | "na bank zaworek. jak jest nieszczelny to będzie za wysoki poziom paliwa" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=3998 |
| 7 | Nysa, carburettor not stated, stored 8 years (previously LPG), 2021 | Starts, runs about 10 s, dies. Keeps running if fuel is poured down the throat. Float, needle and pump diaphragms had already been replaced. | Suggestions: (a) cracked carburettor body, common on LPG S-21s; (b) Marian had identical symptoms on a Żuk and cured them **with a different fuel pump**. Outcome not reported. | "Pompa paliwa sie zawieszala"; "gaźnik jest pęknięty" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=5136 |
| 8 | Żuk 1990, Weber (unspecified twin-choke), 2018 | After disassembly the carburettor no longer worked right. A small choke-area plunger and spring kept popping out. | Found the missing retainer for about 5 zł. Solved. | "wyskakuje mi ten tłoczek"; "Sprawa załatwiona koszt 5 zł" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=16&t=3995 |
| 9 | Żuk A03 1961 with Weber, 2024 | A part was missing in the float-chamber area. | Identified as the **accelerator-pump nozzle, "fi 80 with a ball"**. | "dysza pompki przyspieszenia, fi80 z kulką" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=15&t=5488 |
| 10 | Nysa, Weber-type manifold, 2013 | No start after an engine reseal: wet plugs, bangs, a silencer blown apart. | Not the carburettor. **Ignition timing was lost** when the fuel pump and drive were refitted, and resetting it cured the no-start. | "Kolejność zapłonu 1-2-4-3, sprawdź to najpierw" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=2247 |
| 11 | Żuk A15B S-21, "WEBER", 2020 | Acrid puffs and sticky soot from the air filter housing. | Advice was to start with the crankcase breather (odma) system. A hose-routing photo was posted. No resolution. | "od tej odmy zaczniemy" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=4713 |
| 12 | Żuk S-21, **Jikov** (sister carburettor), 2016 | Hesitation and stutter when pulling away. | Cleaned the jets, confirmed the **ball under the accelerator-pump tube**, raised the float slightly, and replaced the perished **accelerator-pump diaphragm**. Runs better. | "kulka jest pod rurka od pompki przśpieszającej" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=3139 |
| 13 | Same thread, Strucelsw | Uneven response. | Wrong idle mixture or a leaking needle, "common in these carburettors". Fit a needle with a spring damper, set the fuel level, then the idle. | "nieszczelna iglica-czeste w tych gaźnikach" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=3139 |
| 14 | Żuk ("Lodowy Żuczek"), 2019 | Runs on choke when cold, then dies. | Floods (choke not releasing, or float too high), ignition, or the elektrozawór. | "ssanie nie odpuszcza do końca lub pływak za wysoko ustawiony" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=16&t=4392 |

The 34 runs a bit better, especially on LPG, but can have a small flat spot when the pedal is stamped at low rpm. He had one regenerated unit for sale at 300 zł. | "przy depnieciu z małych obrotów" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=3&t=4733 |
| 16 | Weber 28/35 S2A on a Żuk, 2021 | One chamber floods constantly. | Caused by a **broken small spring**. The owner had ordered a repair kit. | "pękła sprężynka i jedna komora jest cały czas zalewana" | https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=3&t=5058 |

### Excluded / caution

- **zukiworld.com "Weber problems" (2011)** is about a Suzuki ("Zuk", Samurai) with a Weber 32/36, not the Polish Żuk. Its fuel-pressure figures (about 1–3 psi) are not S-21 data. https://www.zukiworld.com/forum/technical-discussion-beginner-repair/weber-problems/
- **elektroda.pl topic1869751** is a Piaggio scooter with a Weber that would not start after cleaning. It contains generic advice only. https://www.elektroda.pl/rtvforum/topic1869751.html
- **Mixture-screw direction.** In t=4635 Marian wrote "śrubkę składu musisz wykręcić" (to lean). The OP disputed it, and Strucelsw's test in t=4814 implies that screwing **in** leans the mixture. Treat the t=4635 statement as an error.
