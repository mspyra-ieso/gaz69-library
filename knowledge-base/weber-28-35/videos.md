# Videos: Weber 28/35, its relatives, and the S-21

No video found walks through a **Weber 28/35 S2A1** strip-down or adjustment. The closest material is a Żuk idle clip on a named 28/35 S2A, and Polish videos of the Fiat 125p and Polonez Webers. Those share the FOS/Polmo design family, the EZ-20 solenoid and the idle-jet holder, but **not the jet sizes**.

Titles, channels, dates and descriptions come from the YouTube pages on 2026-10-28. **I have not watched them.** The "why useful" notes are based on the title and description only.
