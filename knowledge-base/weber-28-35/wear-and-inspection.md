# Weber 28/35: wear points, how to inspect, and repair or replace

Labels: [FACTORY], [BOOK], [FORUM], [SHOP], [GENERAL]. Part numbers are from the 1988 Żuk parts catalogue (CAT88, table 11.07): https://archive.org/download/zukowka-zuk-spare-parts-catalog/FSC_LUBLIN-1988-katalog_czesci_zamiennych_samochodu_Zuk.pdf

**No factory wear limits** (in mm) for any 28/35 part were found in Z89, Z94, WZ or CAT88. Where a number appears below it is general carburettor practice and is marked as such.

## 1. Throttle spindles and their bores (the main long-term wear point)

**Why it matters.** Air leaking past a worn spindle enters **below** the venturi. It leans the idle and progression mixture and makes the idle unstable. Owners describe it as "ciągnie fałszywe powietrze" (it draws false air).
- [FORUM] nysanek and Red (FPD t=995, 2010) said this about Jikovs on Nysas: with a worn spindle "będziesz regulować nawet 1 tydzień i nie ustawisz" (you can adjust for a week and never get it set). https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=5&t=995
- The same physics applies to the Weber [GENERAL]. Moss Motoring notes that rough idle, poor running and poor economy result from worn bodies and shafts. https://mossmotoring.com/rebushing-su-carburetor-bodies/

**What owners report on the 28/35:**
- [FORUM] Strucelsw (FPD t=2916, 2015): "te gaźniki szybko łapią luzy" (these carbs wear loose quickly), whether run on petrol or LPG.
  - If the original spindle was **brass**, the spindle is what wears ("wyciera się"). Fit a new **steel** spindle; "korpus z reguły przeżywa" (the body usually survives).
  - Sometimes you just ream the body and fit an **oversize spindle without bushing**.
  - https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=16&t=2916&p=38834
- [FORUM] Juzef (FPD t=777, 2009) had four carbs to choose from and **rejected the factory one** because of noticeable throttle play: "na przepustnicy był luz wyczuwalny". Search snippet: https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/search.php?keywords=luz+przepustnicy&sr=posts
- [FORUM] nysanek's Nysa fault list (FPD t=27, 2007) gives "wyrobiony gaźnik" (worn-out carb) as a cause of excessive throttle opening and rough running. https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=27
- [FORUM] Strucelsw does **not** repair spindle play himself: "nie regeneruję – w sensie naprawy luzów przepustnicy" (FPD t=4733, 2020). He verifies, sets up and cleans. https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=3&t=4733

**Factory provision for wear.** [FACTORY] CAT88 lists **oversize spindles** [oś przepustnicy nadwymiarowa]:

- The large repair kit **CZ-37.29** included spindles with the "x" (oversize) mark ("62x, 39x"). So the factory route was **ream the body and fit oversize spindles**, not bushing.
- [SHOP] The current Art-Mobil kit at roda24 contains an **oversize 8.2 mm primary spindle with plate** (GN.17.17 / 014139). https://sklep.roda24.pl/zestaw-naprawczy-gaznika-28-35-s2a1-zuk-nysa-fso-9354.html
  - The 8.2 mm figure is from the seller; the standard diameter is not stated anywhere found. It is presumably 8.0 mm, but that is **unverified**.

### How to inspect
1. Carb off, plates fitted. **Rock each spindle** up/down and sideways at both ends, and feel for clicks.
2. [GENERAL] More than about **0.005 in (0.13 mm)** of clearance "seriously" affects running (Moss Motoring, SU carbs; not Weber data).
3. **On the engine, at warm idle:** spray carb cleaner or a little petrol at each spindle end. A change in rpm means a leak [GENERAL, Moss]. Do this with care near a hot exhaust.
4. Look for a **polished oval** on the spindle at the bore and for a **brass-coloured** spindle (Strucelsw: brass ones wear).

### Repair options

| Option | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Live with it | Slight play, idle still settles | Keep the idle slightly richer [GENERAL] |
| New standard spindle | Spindle worn, bore round | [FORUM] Strucelsw: body usually survives |
| Ream the bore and fit an oversize spindle (8.2 mm kit) | Bore worn oval | [FACTORY] CAT88 oversize parts; [SHOP] roda24 kit. The two bores must stay aligned. Moss suggests boring from one setting (lathe or jig). https://mossmotoring.com/rebushing-su-carburetor-bodies/ |
| Bushing [tulejowanie] | Bore badly worn | [FORUM] usually costs more than a good used carb (Strucelsw, t=2916). Owner kubamytab planned "oddam do tulejowania" (I'll send it for bushing) if needed |
| Sealed miniature bearings | Specialist | [GENERAL] A Stromberg / Weber DG technique; not reported on the 28/35. https://mikescarbtechnical.notion.site/Worn-throttle-shaft-Stromberg-WW-1ec1c97f2e2a801eaa0dc5d12a2800af |
| Exchange regenerated carb | Any of the above, if you want a known-good unit | [SHOP] 800–950 zł exchange (`rebuild-kits.md`) |

## 2. Warped cover or base flange (lapping, [planowanie / docieranie płaszczyzny])

- **Owner practice on S-21 GAZ-69s [FORUM]:** both Kyiv-region GAZ-69 S-21 logbooks record **lapping the carb flange** during refits:
  - "промыл карбюратор, притер его плоскость" (washed the carb, lapped its face): https://www.drive2.ru/l/573142427284540104/
  - "Притер плоскость прилегания" (lapped the mating face), when fitting a replacement carb: https://www.drive2.ru/l/584099610411147866/
- **Polish forum:** no thread specific to the 28/35 was found. The FPD searches for "planowa*" returned engine-head topics only.
- **How [GENERAL]:**
  1. Tape 240–400-grit wet-and-dry paper to thick glass or a surface plate.
  2. Lubricate it with petrol, WD-40 or water.
  3. Rub in figure-eights, turning the part often, until the low spots disappear.
  4. Check with a straightedge and a 0.05 mm (0.002 in) feeler gauge.

  Owners call it effective but tedious (fiatforum, Weber IMB). https://www.fiatforum.com/threads/weber-26-28-imb.355169/page-3
- **Cautions:**
  - remove the minimum of material;
  - strip **all** jets, the needle valve and the float first;
  - wash the abrasive out of every passage;
  - never lap across a raised boss that locates the gasket.
  - On the cover, check that the float level is re-set afterwards: the float is measured from the cover face (`adjustment-procedures.md` §1). This is an inference.
- **Cause:** over-tightened cover screws, or nuts on a hot manifold [GENERAL]; see the cracked-spacer case in `overhaul-guide.md` §6.
- **Replace instead:** if the cover is cracked or the hinge posts are broken. [SHOP] A new 28/35 cover costs 200 zł at ETER-MOT (`rebuild-kits.md`, `interchangeability.md`).

## 3. Float [pływak] and needle valve [zawór iglicowy]

- **Spec [FACTORY]:**
  - float 9911187 weighs **18 ±0.5 g** (Z94; WZ 17.5–18.5 g);
  - needle valve 175 (1.75 mm seat), part 9911186;
  - level washers 0.5 / 0.7 / 1.0 mm (CAT88 item 10).
- **Float checks:**
  - **Weigh it.** Heavier than 18.5 g means fuel inside. One forum float weighed 24 g and was full of fuel (O12 in `owner-reports.md`).
  - **Shake it** and listen for liquid.
  - **Check the hinge tongue and pin hole** for a groove.
  - [FORUM] Strucelsw: floats **wear through** when the lower stop is not set (FPD t=4717). https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=4717
- **Needle-valve checks:**
  - look for a ridge on the needle's sealing face;
  - the damper ball should spring back;
  - suck or blow through it with the float raised: it should seal.
  - [FORUM] Leaking needles are common. Prefer one with a spring-loaded ball (Strucelsw, t=3139, already cited in `rebuild-kits.md`).
- **⚠ Conflict.** Strucelsw (FPD t=777, 2016) suggests buying "a needle valve from a Solex, with a spring, 2 mm flow", and fitting a **plastic float**. https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=777&p=43092
  - The snippet does not make clear which carb he meant.
  - The 28/35 factory seat is **1.75 mm**. A 2 mm seat raises the flow, so the level must then be re-set.
  - Treat this as an owner modification, not a factory part.
- **Repair or replace:** a sound float can be re-set by bending the tongue. **A float with fuel inside: replace it** (don't solder it unless you can then match the weight). Replace the needle valve at every overhaul; it is in the factory medium kit CZ-37.19.

## 4. Accelerator pump: a piston, not a diaphragm

- **[FACTORY] CAT88:** the pump is a **piston assembly [tłok pompki kpl.] 9911190** with inlet valve 80 (008523) and pump jet 40 (76408040) with a ball valve. **No pump diaphragm [membrana] is listed** for the 28/35 or the S2B1 (BI11).
- **Searches for "membrana pompki przyspieszacza żuk"** led to the **Jikov** (FPD t=5490: "pęknięta membranka od pompki przyspieszenia – taka pod pokrywką na 3 śruby", a cracked pump diaphragm under a 3-screw cover). That is a different carb.
- **⚠** Kit photos showing three diaphragms (ETER-MOT, `rebuild-kits.md`) are probably generic.
- **Inspect:**
  - the piston's leather or rubber cup should be supple and not swollen [GENERAL];
  - the piston should slide without binding;
  - the inlet ball should rattle;
  - with the cover fitted and the bowl full, each throttle stroke should give a crisp jet into throat I. Output is 5 cm³ per 10 strokes (Z94, WZ).
- **Replace** the piston if the cup is hard, cracked or swollen (fuel blends with ethanol can affect it [GENERAL]). Part 9911190 (CAT88 item 19) is in **none** of the three factory CZ-37 kits, so it was a separate spare. Check that any modern kit actually contains it.
- Jikov diaphragm thread: https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=5490

## 5. Jets [dysze]

- **[BOOK]** No steel objects in jets (WZ). Clean them with solvent, compressed air, or a **wooden toothpick / nylon bristle** [GENERAL].
- **Wear** is slow on brass jets [GENERAL]. The common problems are:
  - **wrong jets** fitted by previous owners: 118 instead of 110, a 126p-type 50 instead of 45 (`specs-and-jets.md`, conflicts section);
  - deposits ("zarośnięte takim piaskiem – delikatny kamień", grown over with a fine stony sand; kubamytab, FPD t=2916). These cleaned off and the jets were reused.
- **Replace** a jet when the stamp is unreadable, it has been drilled or reamed, or the orifice is visibly oval. **Never "match" a jet size by drilling** [GENERAL].

## 6. EZ-20 solenoid
Testing, wiring, the get-you-home fix and substitutes are all in `solenoid-ez20.md`. Wear-related points:
- **Ring 033844** is a wear item (factory small kit).
- The solenoids are getting scarce ("towar deficytowy"; Marian, FPD t=4635).
- **Do not** put the solenoid in an ultrasonic bath or solvent soak (inference, `overhaul-guide.md` §4).

## 7. Cold-start (starter) piston [zawór rozruchowy]

- **Parts [FACTORY]:** valve 9910631, spring 9907273, spring cup 9909145, starter cover S28.02.00 (S2B1: S2B.02.00), jets F1/150 and 150.
- **Wear:**
  - the piston's seat face;
  - sticking from varnish;
  - a stretched or weak return spring;
  - a cable that doesn't let the lever return fully: "jazda na półssaniu" (driving on half-choke; nysanek, FPD t=777, 2009, quoted in a search snippet).
- **Inspect:** the piston must slide freely and close fully. With the starter lever off, no fuel should be drawn through the starter circuit (rich when warm otherwise).

## 8. Gaskets, washers and the insulating spacer

- **[FACTORY]** Factory kits:
  - **CZ-37.09** small kit: cover gasket, filter gasket, needle washers, jet washers, pump-valve washers, solenoid ring;
  - **CZ-37.19** medium kit: + filter, needle valve, float, pin;
  - **CZ-37.29** large kit: + jets, emulsion tubes, plates, oversize spindles, springs, solenoid.

  None of these "CZ-37" kits was found on sale today; see `rebuild-kits.md` for current equivalents.
- **Base stack:** gasket 4127681 ×2 and insulating spacer 4127682 (CAT88 table 11.10).
  - [FORUM, drive2] A cracked and bent textolite spacer caused a lean mixture and detonation on a GAZ-69 S-21. https://www.drive2.ru/l/590869853259170000/
  - Inspect the spacer for cracks and bow at every overhaul.
- **Renew all paper and fibre gaskets and washers** every time [GENERAL]. Old cover gaskets shrink and tear.

## 9. Body and casting

- Look for:
  - **stripped threads** at the cover screws and manifold studs;
  - **cracks** around the studs;
  - **white powdery corrosion** in the float bowl, which may mean a zinc-alloy casting (see the ultrasonic cautions in `overhaul-guide.md` §4);
  - **seeping brass plugs**.
- Small stripped threads can take a thread insert [GENERAL]. A cracked body means exchange.

## When is the carb simply worn out?

Signs reported by owners, with the usual verdict:
- **Play in both spindles plus an oval bore:** exchange, or oversize spindles if you have the tools ([FORUM] Strucelsw).
- **Cracked body or cover hinge posts:** exchange, or a new cover ([SHOP] ETER-MOT).
- **Everything else** (dirt, float, needle, jets, gaskets, solenoid) is a normal rebuild.
