## Books

- https://archive.org/download/zukowka-zuk-spare-parts-catalog/ZUK-BEN-angielski.pdf and https://archive.org/download/zukowka-zuk-spare-parts-catalog/ZUK-BEN-angielski_djvu.txt — BOOK (key **WZ**). Wiejak & Zagaja, *Żuk – budowa, eksploatacja, naprawa* (WKŁ 1992), unofficial English translation with the original Polish figure captions. Contents used:
  - Figs 2.23–2.27 (28/35 S2A1 circuits) and Table 2.18 (S2A1 / S2B1 / 34S2C16);
  - Figs 2.49–2.50 (float, dismantling) and 2.55 (level gauge);
  - mounting, PCV valve, wiring-diagram legends showing the "elektrozawór gaźnika".
- Not found online: K. Trzeciak, *Gaźniki* (1989); a separate "Naprawa samochodu Żuk" repair manual; M. Lubczyński, *Naprawa samochodów Nysa*; E. Bańczak, *Naprawa samochodów Nysa 501*. The last two are credited only as image sources on PD.
