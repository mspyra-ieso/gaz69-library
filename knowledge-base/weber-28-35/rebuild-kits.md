# Rebuild kits, gaskets, solenoids and replacement carburettors for the Weber 28/35

Prices are as shown on the pages on **27–28 October 2026** unless dated otherwise. Nothing was bought and no account was used. Check the casting on your carburettor first: **28/35 S2A, S2A1 or S2B1**, and "GN.1113" on S2A bodies (roda24). Kits for the **34 S2A1 / 34 S2B1** are a different carburettor and do not fit.

## Recommendation

For a carburettor that someone has already "cleaned" and that now runs badly, buy:
1. The **ETER-MOT 28/35 S2A1 repair kit (320 zł)**, which is the only one found with gaskets, diaphragms and small parts together. Confirm with the seller whether it fits S2A/S2B1 and what exactly is in it, because the photo also shows floats although the title says "no float".
2. Plus a **float** only if yours is heavier than 18 g or sloshes.
3. Plus an **EZ-20 solenoid** only if yours fails the click or stall tests.

The roda24/Art-Mobil kit (135 zł) is a good **hard-parts** add-on if the throttle spindle is worn. It contains no gaskets.

- **[DOC]** The 28/35 uses solenoid part 033845 with idle jet **45** (033842) and sealing ring 033844 (`specs-and-jets.md`).

## Quality warnings

1. **Kit photos are not always the contents.** The ETER-MOT photo shows floats for a "no float" kit. Ask before buying.
2. **Art-Mobil jets.** The 125p version of the same kit carries 125p jet sizes. Read the stamp on every jet before fitting, against `specs-and-jets.md`.
3. **Floats.**
   - The factory float weighs **18 g** (part 9911187); a 24 g float was full of fuel (O12).
   - On LPG cars floats wear through when the lower stop is not set (Strucelsw, https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=4717).
   - Plastic floats can absorb fuel (https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?p=44947).
4. **Needle valves.** Prefer one with a spring-damper ball; leaking needles are common (Strucelsw, https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=3139).
5. **"New" carburettors** can contain preservative in the float chamber (O25), so flush before fitting.
6. **ST vs NT labels** (old and new type) differ between shops and are not explained. Quote the casting number when ordering.
7. **Solenoid jet.** A new or regenerated 126p/PN EZ-20 may carry the wrong jet for the Żuk (step 9 of the checklist).
