## 2. Why the S-21 moved from the 34S2B1 to the 28/35

- **[FACTORY]** The 1991 bulletin calls the S2B1 "nowy, oszczędny gaźnik" (a new economical carb). The S2B1 is the 28/35 with leaner secondary jets and an economiser. Economy was the stated goal of the 28/35 line.
- **[FORUM] Strucelsw (FPD t=4279):** the 28/35 has a small 21 mm first venturi "aby zdynamizować przepływ, poprawić wymieszanie paliwa i zmniejszyć spalanie" (to speed up the flow, improve mixing and cut consumption) at low load. It is a little more flexible than 2 × 25 mm but loses power at the top end. https://forum.polskiedostawczaki.pl/viewtopic.php?f=6&t=4279
- **[FACTORY]** The EZ-20 solenoid was added at the same time. Z89 explains it as preventing run-on after switch-off.
  - [FORUM] nysanek links run-on to the old blue 78-octane petrol on hot days. He thinks that on today's 95 a working solenoid is less critical (t=2439, 2015).
- **[GENERAL] Inference:** the switch happened amid the 1980s Polish fuel shortages and rationing. No document found states that as the reason.

## 3. The Weber licence in Poland

- **What is documented:**
  - FSO built the Polski Fiat 125p from 1967 under a Fiat licence, with Fiat 1300/1500 engines (Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polski_Fiat_125p).
  - Those Fiat engines used Weber 34 DCHD-type carbs. Polish production became the Polmo / FOS **34 DCMP** family.
    - Mecatechnic sells a Weber 34 DCHD as a replacement for the "Polmo 34 DCMP" of the 1980–86 FSO pick-up (search result, page not opened). https://www.mecatechnic.com/en-GB/p/weber-34-dchd-carburettor-for-fso-pick-up-1980-86-with-1481-cm3_CAR0199
  - The Żuk Webers (34S2B1, 28/35 S2A1 / S2B1) belong to the same Polish "S2" body family. They share covers and levers with the 34 DCHD / DCMP (`interchangeability.md` §1).
- **Markings:** sellers describe carbs marked "Made in Poland lic. Weber typ 34S2C" (search-result snippet of sprzedajemy.pl listings, not opened). The cast text on the ETER-MOT cover photo includes "WEBER" (`images/w-etermot-shared-cover-28-35-34dchd-34dcmp.jpg`).
- **Not found:** the licence contract, its date, its terms, or whether it covered the S2 types specifically. No Polish history source on this was reachable in the time available. **Gap.**

## 4. FOS Łódź versus Polmo Łomianki

| Maker | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **FOS Łódź** (Fabryka Osprzętu Samochodowego, a POLMO group plant) | [FACTORY] The 1985 Żuk manual names "FOS-Łódź" as the maker of the 34S2B1. roda24 lists the plain idle jet "45 FSO, PN, 126p" as original **FOS Łódź** (`rebuild-kits.md`). ETER-MOT lists many Weber 30 S2HX / S2HR (Fiat 126p) items as "FOS Łódź". https://eter-mot.abc24.pl/default.asp?kat=-3&klucz=S2 | High for the 34S2B1 and 126p Webers |
| **Polmo Łomianki** | [SHOP] roda24 names Polmo Łomianki S.A. as the maker of the 28/35 S2A "GN.1113" (`variants.md`). The firm itself (founded 1938 as Dołęgowski & Jezierski) today describes itself as a sintered-metal and plastics moulding company (aplikuj.pl profile), with no mention of carbs. https://www.aplikuj.pl/pracodawca/389762/polmo-lomianki-s-a | Low–medium |
| Neither | CAT88, the bulletins and Z89 / Z94 do **not** name the 28/35 maker | — |

**Best reading:**
- The 28/35 is a member of the FOS Łódź Weber-licence family: shared covers, levers and gaskets with the FOS 34 DCMP / 34S2B1.
- Polmo Łomianki may have built or assembled it later, or made sub-components (sintered or cast parts).
- **Unresolved.** No source states that FOS Łódź made the 28/35, and only one shop states Polmo Łomianki.

## 5. Markings: "GN.1113", "ver.", "ST / NT"

- **GN.1113:** the only source is a roda24 listing and photo (`variants.md`, `images/t-28-35-s2a-gn1113-top-photo-roda24.jpg`). The meaning is unknown.
  - The Art-Mobil spindle "GN.17.17" (roda24 kit) uses the same "GN." prefix, which suggests it is a **maker's or regenerator's drawing or catalogue number**, not a carb type. This is inference only.
- **"ver.1 / ver.3" stamped on the body:** a single zlombol.pl claim, unconfirmed (`variants.md` §3).
- **ST / NT** ("stary typ" / "nowy typ", old / new type): used by ETER-MOT, e.g. "28/35 S2A1 (Żuk) ST" and the cover "Żuk (ST i NT)". Not explained by the seller.
  - It may map to S2A1 versus S2B1, or to early versus late covers. **Ask the seller** and quote the casting.
